RentGuard

Real-time compliance monitoring for New York City short-term rentals. Every listing streams through Kafka, every decision is written to a signed, hash-chained audit ledger you can verify right here, and an AI assistant answers inspectors' questions without being allowed to invent a number.

The audit ledger: check it yourself

Each snapshot decision is one block: the decision plus every listing whose status changed, hashed into a Merkle root, chained to the previous block and signed with ECDSA P-256. Pick a listing and your browser re-checks its receipt with nothing but the public key. Then try editing the record.

    Runs entirely in this page with the Web Crypto API: SHA-256 for the entry, the Merkle path and the block header, then an ECDSA signature check against the service's public key. Nothing is sent anywhere.
